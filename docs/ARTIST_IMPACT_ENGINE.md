# Artist Impact Engine

## Purpose

The Artist Impact Engine adds three strategic questions to the VideoForge Director workflow:

1. Can I scale this?
2. Is this video communicable and socially interactive?
3. What is the artist's call to action?

It does not predict virality or tell an artist whether a creative decision is "good." It surfaces concrete opportunities and evidence so the artist can decide.

## 1. Scale

The engine identifies reusable and extensible elements:
- characters and identity assets
- locations and visual worlds
- recurring motifs
- product/brand integration opportunities
- service or partnership opportunities
- short-form derivatives
- teasers and trailers
- visualizer loops
- lyric-video opportunities
- platform-specific compositions

Every opportunity includes:
- type
- scene/asset reference
- rationale
- integration risk
- artist approval state

Brand/product/service integration is opt-in and rights-aware. The engine should prefer natural story-world placement over intrusive placement.

## 2. Communication and interaction

The engine extracts:
- central message
- emotional promise
- visual hook candidates
- memorable motifs
- quotable or discussion-driving moments
- questions or interaction prompts
- short-form clip candidates
- replay/reveal opportunities

It reports observable signals rather than predicting audience behavior.

Example:
"3 candidate hook moments detected in the first 20 seconds."

Not:
"This will go viral."

## 3. Call to action

The Director Brief supports:
- stream/listen
- follow
- comment
- share
- buy
- visit
- book/contact
- attend
- join
- custom CTA

The chosen CTA becomes a project-level intent and can be attached to selected scenes, end cards, captions, descriptions and derivative exports.

The CTA must remain consistent with the artist's stated intent.

## Impact object

A project stores:
- primary CTA
- secondary CTA
- audience intent
- brand/service/product references
- approved integration points
- hook candidates
- social derivative candidates
- impact review status

## Human gate

The artist/director approves or rejects impact recommendations before they influence the final timeline or export package.
